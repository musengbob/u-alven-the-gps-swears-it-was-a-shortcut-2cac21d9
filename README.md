A one-line entry for the [make.fun bounty "The GPS Swears It Was a Shortcut"](https://make.fun/c/u-alven-the-gps-swears-it-was-a-shortcut-2cac21d9): the GPS, caught red-handed after sending its driver down the wrong road, defends itself in its own smug voice — blaming the map, which promised a shortcut but somehow forgot to mention the lake, the wedding, and every life choice that led the driver to trust it. `main.ts` writes that single line to `entry.txt`.
